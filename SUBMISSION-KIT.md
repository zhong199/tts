# Submission Kit

## Short Description

VoiceForge is a free online AI voice generator and text to speech tool with 20+ neural voices, SSML editing, batch conversion, auto-translation, and downloadable audio.

## Long Description

VoiceForge helps creators, educators, students, accessibility users, and developers convert written text into natural speech. It supports multilingual voices, speed and pitch controls, SSML, batch conversion, browser-local history, and practical workflows for YouTube voiceovers, podcasts, e-learning, Japanese TTS, and developer prototypes.
